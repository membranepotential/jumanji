Document graph — visual reference

The target design of the document graph (t), drawn from its spec. Where this sheet and the spec disagree, the spec wins.

Read with: the feature doc, interaction.md (the interaction model), and DESIGN.md D14 (how it is built).

Sample content: the fictional Lighthouse docs in demo/graph/. Colours are jumanji’s dark theme; yellow circles are annotations, not part of the interface.

a

Links view, near zoom

The graph as it opens on architecture/storage.md, reached from README.md through architecture.md: the defaults of The model.

  1. 1
    The route: one straight accent line from the root to the current node.
  2. 2
    Siblings of a route step: its parent’s other links, in the same column, above (listed before it) and below (after it).
  3. 3
    The current node’s links fan out to its right.
  4. 4
    Handles: +n on a folded node, − on an unfolded one; none without links.
  5. 5
    The selection: a light outline; the panel names it and shows the route.
  6. 6
    A link back to a route node is a child like any other; its accent dot says it is on the route.
b

Hover peek

The pointer rests on the folded node Plugins: its children appear as a temporary fan in a floating layer, and nothing else moves (Gestures, peek).

  1. 1
    The hovered node gets the brighter fill.
  2. 2
    The floating layer: a theme-background backdrop and the fan the unfold would show.
  3. 3
    The scene under the layer is unchanged; leaving the node removes the layer.
c

Tree view with a selection

v shows the spanning tree, every node once; the selection’s link targets are outlined instead of drawn as lines (Visual vocabulary).

  1. 1
    The selection, Configuration keys.
  2. 2
    Its link targets, wherever the tree placed them: an accent outline.
  3. 3
    Everything else off the route is dimmed; no lines are drawn across the tree.
  4. 4
    The route stays straight and at full strength.
d

Far zoom

Zoomed out, the pills give way to 15 px labels cut to their column, leaf runs collapse into bundles, and the route and current node stay legible (P6).

  1. 1
    A bundle bar: three nodes without links of their own. Click it to zoom in on them.
  2. 2
    Labels stay 15 px on screen and end in an ellipsis at their column’s width.
  3. 3
    The route line and the current node’s tint survive every zoom level.
e

Visual vocabulary

Every cue from Visual vocabulary, drawn, with its one meaning (P3).

Accent line
The route.
Accent dot on a node
The node is on the route, also where it appears again.
Accent tint fill
The current node.
Larger dot
The root.
Light outline
The selection.
Brighter fill
Hover.
Handle +n
Folded, with n children.
Handle −
Unfolded.
No handle
No links.
Dashed line
A step no link explains (a jump). Nothing else is dashed.
Bar reading “N nodes”
Zoomed out: N nodes with no links of their own. Click to zoom in.
Accent outline, rest dimmed
Tree view: the selected node’s link targets.
Panel, bottom left
The selection’s title and path; the route as a breadcrumb.
Status line
The view: Graph: links or Graph: tree.
Help line, bottom right
The keys.
Static reference sheet; no scripts. Frames are 1200 × 720 windows at near zoom k = 0.9 and far zoom k = 0.36, with the geometry of src/core/graph/svg.rs.
